# Sample Game Brief: Last Delivery

## Player Fantasy

Be a vulnerable night courier who finishes one dangerous delivery through good judgment rather than combat.

## Player Role

A bicycle courier navigating an unfamiliar apartment block during a power outage.

## Core Loop

Read a delivery clue → choose a dark route → spend limited light to verify landmarks → avoid danger → reach the correct door.

## Primary Mechanic

Aim a battery-limited flashlight to reveal route information and possible threats.

## Target Emotion

Tension created by choosing when uncertainty is safer than spending a scarce resource.

## Win or Completion Condition

Deliver the parcel to the correct apartment and return to the lobby.

## Failure Condition

The courier is caught after revealing or entering an unsafe route.

## Prototype Hypothesis

If players must trade flashlight charge for route certainty, then they will pause and make meaningful route choices, evidenced by distinct light-use decisions and human reports of tension.

## Explicit Prototype Exclusions

Combat, inventory, progression, multiple deliveries, production art, save system, voice acting, and procedural levels.

## Unknowns

Whether the threat is readable enough to feel fair and whether the route choice stays meaningful after one attempt.

## Assumptions

One compact gray-box floor and placeholder audio can test the hypothesis.
