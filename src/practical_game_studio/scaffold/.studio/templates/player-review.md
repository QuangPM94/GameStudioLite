# Player Review

Consumer: `/issue-map` and `/milestone-review`. Validation purpose: classify experience findings without fake playtest claims.

## Review Basis

Select all available: source/specification, screenshots, recorded video, runtime, test logs, telemetry, human playtest notes.

When active observed runtime or observed human-playtest evidence is unavailable, include:

> This is a simulated player-experience review based on available artifacts.
> It is not a substitute for an observed human playtest.

## Player Profiles

Evaluate the relevant profiles separately:

- First-time player: onboarding, affordances, immediate goal, and feedback.
- Genre fan: genre expectations, depth, freshness, and mastery promise.
- Casual player: cognitive load, friction, session fit, and recovery.
- Explorer: curiosity, discovery signals, space, and optional interaction.
- Optimization-focused player: readable rules, trade-offs, mastery, and exploitable ambiguity.
- Skeptical player: early value proof, trust, friction tolerance, and abandonment triggers.

## Findings

Every finding starts with `OBSERVED`, `USER_REPORTED`, `INFERRED`, or `UNKNOWN` and covers relevant clarity, engagement, intended emotion/tension, pacing, feedback, friction, emotional payoff, desire to continue, and stability.
